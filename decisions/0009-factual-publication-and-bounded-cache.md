# 0009 — Interim factual publication and bounded public caching

Status: implemented interim behavior; the full photographed catalog remains required.

The owner requested immediate display of genuine products. Publish source-backed product facts and our own factual summaries separately from media. The facts-only lane exposes no image references, source marketing copy, claimed license or inferred supplier availability. It is not acceptance of a permanently photo-free catalog. Media publication retains its exact candidate/revision and image approval checks.

The owner subsequently required navigation speed to be fixed before expanding catalog/media. Cache only anonymous approved public HTML for at most 60 seconds, with locale/path/query/build partitioning. Bypass auth/cookies, search, API requests, writes, unsupported variants, errors and personalized responses. Keep publication readback APIs uncached. Content withdrawal can take up to the HTML cache TTL to appear on a cached page.

Known curated brand identity should not require another remote request per product card. Fetch independent public projections concurrently. Cache unversioned fonts for one day and existing unversioned editorial WebPs for one hour; reserve immutable caching for content-versioned assets.

Future product photos need approved source/usage evidence and responsive derivatives; do not substitute generated or unrelated pictures. Detailed product zoom must retain source detail while list cards should receive only their display size. No paid image service, account permission expansion or scheduled job was adopted by this decision.

Bounded stale-while-revalidate follow-up on 26 October (owner backlog B1, recorded by the implementation owner under decision 0010): anonymous public HTML stays fresh for 60 seconds after it is stored. From 60 to under 600 seconds after that store time, a colo may serve the stored copy while one background render per page and isolate replaces it. Withdrawn content, or a page that still references a revoked image, can therefore be served from a cached page in each colo for up to 600 seconds after the copy was stored. This applies to the stale request that starts the refresh, to requests that arrive while that refresh runs or while the refresh table is full, and whether or not the refresh succeeds. A refresh that finds the page withdrawn or redirected removes the copy. A failed refresh leaves it until the 600-second bound. A new deploy never reuses an earlier release's copy. Errors, personalized, authenticated and non-allowlisted requests remain uncached. The window is the single `STALE_SECONDS` constant in `apps/storefront/src/server/public-cache.mjs`; shorten it if the owner wants faster withdrawal. This reaches visitors only after an approved Worker deploy.

Delivery addendum on 27 October 2026 (recorded by the implementation owner under decision 0010): product derivatives become lossy WebP with a measured quality search instead of lossless WebP. The binding requirements are unchanged: detailed zoom must retain source detail, list cards receive only their display size, and the 21 October performance checkpoint's budgets apply (about 80–150 KB per card derivative, WebP only after visual comparison, a reviewed exception rather than destructive compression where detail suffers). "Lossless" in the 21 October follow-up described the helper's first implementation; no decision requires it. Measured on the five held 960px renditions of the m126234-0015 sample: lossless WebP made 15 files of 4,525,028 bytes, with all ten 640/960 files over the 150 KiB card budget and the dial's 960px file over the 600 KiB detail budget. The quality ladder (92 down to 75) with floors of card SSIM 0.97 and detail SSIM 0.985 at quality 85 or more, against a same-width Lanczos3 reference, made 1,221,380 bytes: 13 of 15 files fit their budget and floor at quality 92, the dial's shared 960px card/detail file meets its floor only at 187,998 bytes (quality 88) and the box's 960px file misses the detail floor even at quality 92 (172,998 bytes, SSIM 0.98373). Those two stay flagged for a reviewed exception; the gate is not waived. A side-by-side 2x visual comparison of dial text, minute track, bezel fluting and date window showed no visible loss against the reference; fabric texture is slightly smoother. Encoding is deterministic (pinned options; same input, same bytes). No crop, upscale or colour change; EXIF/GPS are stripped and ICC kept. Nothing was published by this change.
