# 0008 — Minimal catalog without an owner dashboard

Status: accepted owner requirement; implementation remains pending

Date: 2026-10-04

The owner clarified that they do not want a product-management dashboard,
to learn the catalog, or to enter/manage individual products. Remove the
separate catalog console from the proposed scope, not merely from public
navigation. Provider dashboards are optional developer tools.

The public product experience is:

`brand → collection → product name → clear actual product images → WhatsApp inquiry`

There are no prices, visitor accounts, cart or checkout. Preserve the accepted
visual identity and category navigation; categories are navigation groupings,
not a replacement for each brand's named collections. Do not expand the
product page into commercial or technical fields the owner did not request.

Codex and Claude prepare product collection, classification, image checks and
updates in the background using reviewed sources and controlled development
tools. The workflow must not depend on the owner operating a console or
approving every ordinary data-entry action. Source, stable references,
permissions and publication/withdrawal records remain internal so imported
content can be verified, deduplicated and removed correctly. Resolve material
missing facts or decisions through the existing conversation.

Supabase remains the proposed database default; its recommendation does not
depend on an owner dashboard or visitor authentication. This correction does
not authorize provisioning, new subscriptions, production publication or
unverified automated scraping. Earlier WhatsApp/social operations remain a
separate workstream rather than fields on the public product page.
